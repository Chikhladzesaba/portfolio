Website Transformation for SEO and Audience Growth

As part of digital transformation process for leading media outlet, a key challenge was fixing the slow web publishing process. Disconnected CMS workflows and inconsistent page layouts hurt SEO and made the site confusing to navigate, which held back audience growth. I worked closely with editorial and digital teams to clean up workflows and improve the site’s structure. By aligning editorial goals with technical improvements, we made publishing faster and smoother. I focused on using data to guide decisions, introduced SEO best practices, and helped content creators and editors collaborate more effectively. We set clear SEO and content guidelines, trained creators on best practices, reorganized key pages based on user behavior, and began A/B testing headlines and content placement. This brought editorial and tech teams together and boosted audience engagement.

Results

By the end of my time there: ⏩ publishing speed had doubled, allowing content to go live faster. 📈 SEO improvements boosted search rankings for key topics, while the user experience improved, with visitors spending more time on the site. 🚀👥 Unique visitors tripled, driving significant audience growth. 🔄📊 Visits and page views increased five times, showing that users were not only coming back but engaging more deeply with the content. 🧰 Tools I used: Chartbeat, Adobe Analytics, Google Search Console; Google ads; Crazy egg;
